Chung-Chin Shih 施仲晉

Assistant Professor, Department of Computer Science and Information Engineering, National Chung Cheng University

I work on computer game theory, game solving, and reinforcement learning methods for board games. My recent research studies how search, learning, and proof techniques can be combined to solve difficult game problems and build stronger game AI systems.
